# E-Commerce Customer Churn & Retention Analysis Dashboard

---

# Project Overview

This project analyzes 50,000 e-commerce customer records to understand customer churn, retention, purchasing behavior, engagement, and potential churn-risk factors.

The dashboard was developed in Microsoft Excel using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPIs, calculated columns, and interactive slicers to convert raw customer data into actionable business insights.

Dataset Link - https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/dhairyajeetsingh/ecommerce-customer-behavior-dataset/data

---

# Business Objective

The objective is to identify why customers may churn, which customer segments have higher churn rates, and what behavioral factors are associated with customer retention.

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Total Customers | Total number of customers |
| Churned Customers | Number of customers who stopped purchasing |
| Retained Customers | Number of customers who continue purchasing |
| Churn Rate | Percentage of customers who churned |
| Average Lifetime Value | Average value generated by a customer |
| Average Order Value | Average value of a customer order |

---
 
# Key Insights
- Overall customer churn is 28.9%, indicating a significant retention opportunity.
- Inactive customers have a much higher churn rate than recent purchasers.
- Customers with low engagement show higher churn compared with highly engaged customers.
- High service interaction is associated with substantially higher churn.
- Customers with high cart abandonment and high return behavior show elevated churn.
- Churned customers have lower login frequency, session duration, purchases, and email engagement than retained customers.
- USA has the highest number of churned customers among the countries analyzed.

---

# Business Questions Answered
- What is the overall customer churn rate?
- Which customer segments have the highest churn?
- How does customer engagement relate to churn?
- Which countries have the highest number of churned customers?
- How does purchase recency affect churn?
- How do cart abandonment and return behavior relate to churn?
- How do churned and retained customers differ in their behavior?

---

# Recommendations
- Develop targeted retention campaigns for inactive and at-risk customers.
- Improve engagement through personalized offers and communication.
- Reduce cart abandonment through better checkout experiences and targeted reminders.
- Monitor customers with frequent service interactions or returns.
- Encourage repeat purchases through loyalty and personalized marketing strategies.
